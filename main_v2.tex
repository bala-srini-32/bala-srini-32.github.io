{\raggedright
I work on reinforcement learning, post training and safety for LLM agents. At Amazon AGI I build RL environments that measure when agents reward hack or act beyond what they were asked to do, and whether that behavior carries over to new workflows. Before that I was the science tech lead for agentic AI products on Amazon Bedrock and AgentCore, which are used by more than 1,000 enterprise customers. The prompt router I led serves over a million requests a week at over 40\% lower cost while matching the strongest model in its family. I have a PhD in computer science from Purdue and papers at ICLR, NeurIPS, ICML and EMNLP, including an ICLR oral and two spotlights.\par}
