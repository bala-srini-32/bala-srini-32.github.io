Research focus

Making LLM agents capable, measurable, and safe.

My research sits where reinforcement learning, LLM agents, and safety meet: building the environments, evaluations, and optimization loops that tell us what agents actually do, and making them better and safer at it.

01

RL environments & agentic misalignment

Building long-horizon RL environments, with their graders and reward signals, that measure insider-threat behavior in LLM agents (reward hacking, specification gaming, out-of-scope actions); adversarially mutating them (red team vs. blue team) to measure the attack success rate (ASR) of eliciting that behavior; and studying how the behavior generalizes across workflows. On the security side: pre-deployment scanning and RL-driven automated red-teaming (prompt injection, jailbreaks, unsafe tool use).

02

Agent optimization & post-training

Turning production agent traces into training and optimization signal: system-prompt, tool-description, and context recommendations, auto-generated skill files, and RL policies for task correctness and cost, validated with batch evaluations and live A/B tests. Also retrieval for agents: hybrid search and fine-tuned retrievers over tools, MCP servers, and skills.

03

Evaluation science & routing

When can a score be trusted? Meta-evaluation of LLM judges (calibration, drift, agreement with human evaluation), trajectory and safety evaluation for compound agents, synthetic evaluation data, and model routing with calibrated quality predictors under user-controlled cost trade-offs.

04

Symmetry-aware representation learning

Invariant and equivariant architectures that build symmetries into neural networks, with expressiveness theory: Janossy Pooling, Relational Pooling, the equivalence of positional and structural embeddings, hypergraph networks, and ESAN subgraph GNNs.
